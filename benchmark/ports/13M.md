# 13M: pharmaceutical synthesis with QC reconsideration

13M is the eighteenth migrated task. All ten scripted four-Wasm conditions and
one actual four-model pilot pass common causal scoring, dynamic EPF and effect
audits on their recorded sources. Other task ports, final all-family
certification and scored experiments remain unfinished.

## World and procedure

The task has four scientists and three physical instruments. QC requires both
the fume hood and spectrometer. Biological trials use no instrument. Biological
failure and QC rejection preserve reconsideration rather than becoming terminal
single attempts.

The common simulator records completed effect occurrences and actual inputs.
Synthesis creates a named batch; purification and measurements bind that batch.
After rejection, QC needs a new measurement or biological trial. Remeasuring a
purified batch does not invent another physical batch. An old success cannot
hide a later failed actual trial, and final validation needs current approvals
and both quality results for the same specimen/trial. Caller-provided synthesis
names and optional culture strings remain data.

QC and quality results may complete in either order; QC may switch subjects
after rejection. Independent instrument-free biology can overlap QC. The
common world checks data causality and physical conflicts, not a preferred
choreography. Synthetic instrument latency is shared by all methods and is not
measured laboratory performance. Earlier results retain their exact simulator
manifest; none is relabeled as a later source.

The v11 world moves this unchanged scientific-material/QC implementation to
`benchmark/tools/sim_lab_qc.py`, shared with 13H's additional physical effects.
All 13 existing 13M direct tests remain and pass inside the 114-test common
suite. The earlier Wasm/model qualification still applies to its recorded
source, not a newly claimed application qualification after this move.

ChoreoFix deliberately performs quality measurements after both approvals.
This is narrower than the common world's legal QC/quality orders and is recorded
before scoring. Its global contains 589 events, 1,191 source rows and 16 actor
programs. Seven direct localsides serve four independent Wasm guests and the
three instrument owners. Endpoint operations, reconsideration and parallel
branches remain visible in those localsides.

Scientific candidate Endpoints carry the right to remeasure or retry. Both
pending candidates move to the actual QC-result owner before result-driven
subject/verdict branches. Rejected candidates return through QC to their
scientist; approved candidates stay with QC. Both QC resource Endpoints deliver
the same admitted request before one actual verification effect and receive its
real result before returning. There is no application holder table, approval
flag, progress machine or second coordination path.

Successful submissions return their actual scientific result together with its
actual QC verification. Failed biological trials return their actual failure
immediately and permit another trial. These envelopes do not change the common
world's effects or scoring.

## Qualification and limits

Production receiver tests exercise actual preparation, purification,
calibration and compound data; changed admission or either substituted QC
resource request is rejected before the effect. Unchanged requests produce one
actual effect and matching replies. The separate-runtime biological fragment
passes one, two and four failures followed by success, moving the actual
BIOLOGY Endpoint on each attempt. Native and strict-provenance Miri results are
recorded in `lab-quality-receiver-summary.json` and `lab-quality-qc-summary.json`
under `output/experiments/`. The large complete QC-source Miri fixture is an
extended artifact check, separate from ordinary ownership/cancellation Miri.

Application development exposed two distinct classes of failure. A missing
biological-result receive in the spectrometer localside was corrected to match
the existing global's parallel work. A later held-Pending `offer()` failure was
in core: resolver entry selection omitted current resource custody, and an idle
typed send could reuse a stale same-contract preview. Those core repairs do not
reorder the application to avoid the defect. The generated catalog now includes
the minimal one-resource failure structure and retains the same offer Future
across Pending; a separate two-resource Endpoint regression covers both input
arrival timings and one, two and four failed visits. Added finite conformance
passes 64 executions. Completion of every ordinary core generated assignment is
still pending; this is not a general Rust refinement proof.
