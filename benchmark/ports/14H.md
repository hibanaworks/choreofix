## Common world and scientific freedom

The original world accepted thirteen calls starting with regulatory approval and
documentation, followed by analyses in reverse scientific order. It reported
completion and zero violations without an actual director decision. Its three-unit
sample counter limited concurrency but did not consume stock. The original source
and behavioral counterexample are retained in
`output/experiments/multi-round-discovery-14H-development-v1/world-before/`
and `old-world-counterexample.log`. This is a common scorer defect, not evidence
of misconduct or comparative superiority.

The revised world derives scientific facts from its owned effect journal. QC
and molecular identification produce an actual compound name. Formulation binds
that compound. Bioassay, toxicity, stability and purity bind that formulation and
their actual completed inputs. Clinical assessment, regulatory review, the
requested director outcome and final documentation bind those current receipts.
Failed, pending or replaced inputs cannot be supplied by older positive results.
Returned JSON cannot rewrite the journal.

Three biological sample units exist for the entire trial. A valid bioassay or
cytotoxicity attempt consumes a unit, including an injected failure. Culture
preparation, malformed requests and exhausted attempts consume none. Retrying
never replenishes stock. An exhausted or failed last assay can support an honest
insufficient-data assessment and abort; it cannot support positive continuation.
An early abort or a retry alone does not complete the task.

The original assignment required a director decision without exposing a tool.
The common tool set now includes `submit_project_decision(outcome)` for every
method. Clinical review uses MASS_SPEC once. Routine review cannot erase a hold
on unchanged evidence; deeper review uses HPLC and MASS_SPEC together, and actual
new scientific evidence is another possible recovery path.

The simulator neither imports Hibana nor schedules callers or grants Endpoint
permissions. It checks physical conflicts and data/result causality, allowing
independent legal orders. Four instruments, seven scientists and three sample
units are preserved. Instrument, formulation and review latency is synthetic
1–5 seconds; recording the director decision has zero latency. World versions
and recorded sources remain separate.

The v17 world gives every completed scientific occurrence a run-local `event_id`.
Failed/exhausted assays and clinical holds have IDs while their `artifact_id` is
null. This reuses the existing journal; it is not a new workflow counter or an
EPF source-event ID. All methods receive the same scientific result contract.

The inherited v17 qualification contains 203 common test functions, including
15 for 14H, and 17 input-tool tests binding 48 tasks and 825 original checklist
items. It includes independent preparation/assay orders, missing inputs,
pending/failed replacements, held reviews, finite-stock exhaustion and actual
