# 15E · Wafer fabrication

The integration engineer receives the reference-wafer Endpoint. Four ChoreoFS
paths on that Endpoint let the wash guest mount the wafer, measure its alignment,
and run the film-integrity and etch-uniformity checks in either order. Once the
stepper has submitted the mount, it lends the stage Endpoint to metrology. The
measurement can start while the mount call is outstanding. Metrology keeps the
stage after a failed measurement and returns it after a successful one.

Three real Wasm runs cover film first, etch first, and a failed overlay
measurement followed by a retry on the same load. They also exercise both
orders of reading the mount and measurement replies. The retry case attempts a
spot check immediately after the failed measurement. The shell receives an
error and can retry the measurement. The audit checks each measurement against
its own received request, simulator invocation, physical result and reply.
Both spot checks name the same mount and successful measurement receipts.

The ordinary quality workflow builds the runtime with `--build-wasi` and runs
`oracle::tests::wash_uses_the_same_reference_wafer_for_both_spot_checks`.
All three common-world scores completed with zero violations.
The [alignment measurements](../../analysis/15e-wafer-alignment.json) give the
actual effect counts, EPF captures, timing and hashes. One scripted wash guest
issued the four integration operations. The preparations for the other five
participants were simulator fixtures. Full six-participant Wasm and live-agent
qualification remain open.
