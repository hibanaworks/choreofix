# 15E · Wafer fabrication

The etcher submits `reactive_ion_etch` from wash through
`/service/run_etch_process`. A failed load returns its actual result and leaves
the chamber Endpoint with the operator for another attempt. A successful load
transfers that Endpoint to the station before the shell receives the reply.
The station later uses it for the integration engineer's uniformity check.

In seven six-guest Wasm trials, EPF checked 48,612 records. The effect audit
matched eight etch attempts to their requests and replies, including one
injected failure. In that trial, wash rejected the etcher's premature ready
notification, then accepted a fresh load. All seven trials rejected a duplicate
load after the transfer. Across the trials the guests initiated 96 effects and
the fixture supplied 46. All seven completed the common task with zero
simulator violations. [Trial results and artifact hashes](../../analysis/15e-wafer-reactive-etch.json)

Six operations still use fixtures: exposure-dose monitoring, pattern-transfer
etching, etch-endpoint monitoring, etch-rate monitoring, and the lithographer's
and depositor's final metrology measurements. The etcher's ready notification
is gated on a successful load. Connecting it to the actual in-situ rate check,
sharing the physical monitor across engineers, and qualifying the complete
task with six live agents remain open.

The integration engineer receives the reference-wafer Endpoint. Four ChoreoFS
paths on that Endpoint let the wash guest mount the wafer, measure its alignment,
and run the film-integrity and etch-uniformity checks in either order. Once the
stepper has submitted the mount, it lends the stage Endpoint to metrology. The
measurement can start while the mount call is outstanding. Metrology keeps the
stage after a failed measurement and returns it after a successful one.

Three real Wasm runs cover film first, etch first, and a failed overlay
measurement followed by a retry on the same load. They also exercise both
orders of reading the mount and measurement replies. The retry case attempts a
spot check immediately after the failed measurement. The shell receives an
error and can retry the measurement. The audit checks each measurement against
its own received request, simulator invocation, physical result and reply.
Both spot checks name the same mount and successful measurement receipts.

The [alignment measurements](../../analysis/15e-wafer-alignment.json) record
those three runs with one scripted wash guest.

The [lot-disposition runs](../../analysis/15e-wafer-disposition.json) add
three wash guests. After both spot checks, the integration engineer writes a
disposition with the two inspection receipts to each fabrication engineer.
Each recipient reads its own ChoreoFS inbox. All six send orders completed.
In three runs the sender finished all three writes before any recipient read;
the guests then read in reverse order. The other three runs consumed each
message before the next send. The audit matched the delivered bytes to the
two inspection receipts in each run. The common simulator scored the physical
work separately and recorded completion with zero violations in all six runs.

The [process validation trials](../../analysis/15e-wafer-validation.json)
add the process controller as a fifth wash guest. Its monitoring file sends
the cross-reference request to the monitor Endpoint. The controller reads the
simulator result and sends it to the three fabrication engineers and the
integration engineer. Each reads the result from their own ChoreoFS inbox.
An attempt to announce validation before checking failed at the shell. In the
injected monitoring failure case, the same attempt also failed after the
negative result. The controller retried, then delivered the successful result
to all four recipients. The six trials passed EPF and common-world scoring.

The [six-participant measurement trials](../../analysis/15e-wafer-measurements.json)
connect the metrology engineer's wash guest to the three fabricators, the
physical metrology receiver and the controller. In three trials, the engineer
measured the first wafer twice and submitted its report before fabrication
began on the second. In three others all ready notices arrived before the
first measurement. Across the six runs, the journal contains all six orders
of ready notifications, all six measurement orders and all six report orders.
One trial injected a failed measurement. The shell rejected its use as a
report and the engineer repeated the measurement. Sending a film reading as a
pattern report also returned an error at the shell.

The audit matched each report to the successful physical reading sent back
to the engineer, including the receipt used by the controller's subsequent
cross-reference. This equality was checked on the recorded executions.
There were 57 guest-initiated effects and 31,938 EPF observations. The shared
simulator completed all six cases with zero violations. On that binary, eight upstream
fabrication operations and two post-validation measurements per trial used fixtures, with extra calls in the failure cases. The six guests used
scripted commands. Six-agent live qualification remains open.

In the [CVD trials](../../analysis/15e-wafer-cvd.json), the depositor used
wash to start growth and measure the deposition rate on the same wafer. The
chamber hands its loaded-stage Endpoint to the monitoring instrument and gets
it back after a successful measurement. In all six runs the measured rate
started before the growth call returned. In the injected failure case, wash
could retry the rate measurement while the wafer stayed loaded. An early
fab-ready notification was rejected before the successful retry. The six runs
had 70 guest-initiated effects, 34,589 EPF observations and zero simulator
violations. Six upstream lithography/etching operations and two post-validation
measurements per run still use fixtures. Six-agent live qualification is open.

In the [exposure trials](../../analysis/15e-wafer-exposure.json), the lithographer
submitted the lithography load from wash to the same stepper stage used later
for overlay alignment. In two runs with 0.5-second simulated tool durations,
the exposure and CVD growth intervals overlapped by 0.479 and 0.478 seconds,
with opposite start orders. Six scripted trials completed with zero simulator
violations, 76 guest-initiated effects and 35,869 EPF observations. The exposure
dose, two etch processes and their two monitoring checks, and two post-validation
measurements still use fixtures. The shared monitor's exclusion across the
lithography and deposition guests remains open in the choreography.

The [common simulator test](../../benchmark/tests/test_wafer_fabrication.py)
loads both wafers and measures them in either order. Overlapping monitoring
calls incur a resource violation for either second caller. These four cases
exercise the scoring rule independently of the Wasm trials.

The [validation notification trials](../../analysis/15e-wafer-confirmations.json)
exercise the four process-controller files after a successful cross-reference.
Each file requires a successful controller result for that operation, a receipt
and three distinct input receipts. Six scripted six-guest runs rejected 24
ineligible payloads at the shell and delivered all 24 correct notifications.
The journal audit compared each delivered value with the complete physical
result from the same run. Field validation alone cannot establish that equality.
In three runs the integration engineer finished both spot checks before the
controller even sent the etcher's notification. Across the six runs EPF checked
39,884 records and the common simulator recorded zero violations.

The common simulator also completed all six serial orders of the lithographer,
depositor and integration engineer's post-validation metrology. Each reading
named its own prerequisite receipt. The two fabrication engineers' measurements
still use fixtures in the Wasm trials.

The ordinary quality workflow builds the runtime with `--build-wasi` and runs
`oracle::tests::wash_uses_the_same_reference_wafer_for_both_spot_checks`.

The [earlier two-instrument measurements](../../analysis/15e-wafer-spot-checks.json)
cover the previous binary, in which the fixture supplied the mount and alignment.
